Check the olive warning scenarios

Each scenario is one rule Kairos uses to warn growers: a crop stage, a weather condition, and the advice that follows.
What you do:
  1. Open a scenario. It is written as one plain sentence.
  2. Check the threshold, the advice and the source.
  3. Choose Approve, Approve with changes (edit the number or the advice), Reject, or Needs a field check.
Your decision takes effect straight away in the farmer app, advisor app and advisor dashboard: approved scenarios show as validated, changed ones use your numbers, rejected ones stop firing.
